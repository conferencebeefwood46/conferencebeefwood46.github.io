🤔 What Is mlx-lean-moe?

This tool lets you talk to a very smart AI assistant right on your Mac, without needing an internet connection and without hogging all your computer's memory. It uses a clever method called "Mixture of Experts" (MoE) toonly load the tiny, most relevant parts of the AI model when you need them, rather than loading everything at once. Think of it like a huge library where an assistant fetches only the exact books you need from a remote storage room, instead of carrying every book into your reading room.

Because it streams (loads) only what is required from your disk drive, the total memory usage stays incredibly low. This means you can run a powerful 3B or even larger Qwen3 model on a MacBook with just 8GB of RAM, all while keeping other apps open smoothly. Everything runs privately on-device — your chats never leave your machine.

⚙️ Under The Hood (For The Curious)

Technically speaking, this project leverages a Mixture-of-Experts architecture deployed via mlx.core, Apple's machine learning framework. Traditional AI models load all their "neurons" into RAM during inference, which is wasteful because for any given prompt, only a few "expert" pathways are actually used. mlx-lean-moe exploits this by keeping all expert weights on your solid-state drive (SSD) and streaming only relevantones into memory on demand. This achieves memory efficiency without sacrificing response quality.
